# Project: github-portfolio (natadecua)

## Design Context

### Users
**Primary audience: hiring managers and senior engineers / tech leads.** They arrive cold, often from a resume link or LinkedIn, and decide in roughly 10 seconds whether to keep reading. They are not looking for an art piece; they are looking for *signal* — what does this person build, how often, in what languages, with what depth. Visual ambition has to serve that scan, not replace it.

Context of use: desktop on a work machine during a hiring screen, occasionally mobile during a commute. Reduced-motion users are common in this audience.

Secondary audience: fellow engineers who land here from a repo link and will tolerate more depth.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **technical, raw, honest** — overlaid with **precise, quiet, confident**.

The portfolio represents the work, not a persona. It should read like someone who would rather show the source than the slide deck. No founder-grin, no marketing voice, no "I help teams ship". Body copy is sparse and factual. The flex is in restraint and in the craft of the specimens, not in adjectives.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Editorial Brutalism × Spatial — "Floating Specimens"** direction (confirmed during brainstorming).

- **Surface:** off-white paper in light mode (around `oklch(0.97 0.005 80)`), deep ink in dark mode (around `oklch(0.16 0.01 80)`). Both modes tinted slightly warm — not the cool Vercel/shadcn slate. System-driven via `prefers-color-scheme`, plus a manual override.
- **Type:** distinctive display face paired with a refined neutral body. **Banned fonts:** Inter, Roboto, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta, IBM Plex (any), Space Grotesk, Space Mono, Fraunces, Instrument Sans/Serif, Outfit. Look further — candidates worth evaluating include Söhne, Neue Haas Grotesk, ABC Diatype, GT America, PP Neue Montreal, PP Editorial New, JetBrains Mono only if used for actual code (never as a vibe signal).
- **Accent:** exactly one. A warm vermillion / signal-orange in the `oklch(0.65 0.22 35)` range. Used sparingly — full-stops, focus rings, hover states, the dot on the live-status indicator. Never on body text, never as a gradient, never on hero text.
- **Layout:** hairlines instead of borders, generous gaps instead of card containers. No glass, no neon glow, no gradient text, no left-stripe alerts.
- **WebGL:** purposeful, never ambient. Each repo gets a *unique generative specimen* — seeded by repo name, language, age, and star count — rendered with React Three Fiber. Specimens parallax gently on scroll. There is **no** atmospheric hero shader, **no** particles, **no** floating blobs in the background.

**Anti-references (must not resemble):**
- Generic Vercel / shadcn dark dashboard aesthetic (slate bg, cyan accent, glass cards, gradient hero text).
- Awwwards-agency over-design (cursor trails, every-element-animated, blocking intro loaders).
- Corporate SaaS landing pages (hero-with-screenshot, three-column features, testimonial grid).

### Design Principles

1. **Signal in the first viewport.** A recruiter must learn within 10 seconds: who, what languages, how active, what's worth clicking. The hero earns its space only if it delivers this. Decoration that delays signal is cut.

2. **Restraint is the flex.** One accent color, one display font, one body font, hairlines not borders, one piece of motion per moment. Anything that would not be missed if removed *should* be removed.

3. **Every specimen is data.** Generative 3D objects are derived from real repo facts (language → palette role, stars → density, commits → motion). Never a decorative placeholder. If the data can't drive it, it doesn't ship.

4. **Quiet, not slow.** The page is calm visually but instantaneous mechanically. No intro loader. No blocking animation. View transitions and parallax are short (≤300ms) and exponential-eased. Reduced-motion users get a fully static version that loses nothing structural.

5. **WebGL serves the work, not the portfolio.** If a user disables JS or has a weak GPU, the layout still reads end-to-end. Specimens are progressive enhancement, not the content.
